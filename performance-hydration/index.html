PLYT Field Notes / Hydration

What does performance hydration actually mean?

For a short, easy session, water is often enough. Performance hydration is the broader idea of planning fluids around longer, hotter or more demanding activity—without turning a simple decision into a science project.

Start with the session.

The useful question is not “what is the best hydration product?” It is “what does this session ask of me?” Duration, heat, sweat, access to water and personal preference all change the answer. A long outdoor run is different from an easy walk. A hard gym session is different from a tournament day.

Why convenience matters.

Hydration plans fail when they are difficult to carry, mix or remember. That is why portable formats can be useful: they make it easier to prepare a bottle when the session is likely to run long.

PLYT’s approach: make the choice straightforward. When a normal bottle of water is not enough for the demands of the session, PLYT aims to be an easy option to reach for.

Keep claims in perspective.

Hydration products are not a substitute for eating well, listening to your body or seeking professional advice for individual health needs. Product ingredients, quantities and use guidance should always come from final packaging and qualified professionals.
