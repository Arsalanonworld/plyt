What is PLYT IGNITE?

IGNITE is PLYT’s Lemon Mint single-serve performance hydration powder. It is currently in development for longer, more demanding physical sessions.

Can I buy it today?

Not yet. We are not taking orders until the product is ready. The launch list is for updates and potential early access.

What will be in the final product?

Final formulation, ingredient quantities and packaging claims are still being validated. We’ll only publish those details once they are final and appropriately reviewed.
